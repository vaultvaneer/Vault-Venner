Vault & Veneer is a bespoke interior architecture and turnkey design studio founded by Wanya Haider in Kolkata. Dedicated to creating calm, enduring spaces characterized by spatial clarity, tactile materiality, and refined botanical accents, the studio delivers comprehensive design and execution across discerning residential and commercial environments.

Our practice unites rigorous space planning, handcrafted millwork, and refined architectural lighting with tactile natural materials. From initial concept drawings and site coordination to turnkey handover, each project is crafted to balance aesthetic restraint with effortless daily utility.
